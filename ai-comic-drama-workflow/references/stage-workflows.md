# 13-Phase Workflow

1. Project setup and source ingest: immutable copies, hashes, entry mode, work depth, adaptation policy.
2. Intake and gaps: readable content, rights, conflicts, missing evidence.
3. Narrative and dynamic memory: source facts and chapter-batch state.
4. Adaptation plan: approved boundaries and beats.
5. Screenplay: observable scenes, dialogue, and enhancement.
6. Story review and GenerationSegments: explicit 10/15/20-second production grouping.
7. Visual assets: style, character, scene, and prop definitions; reference images or prompts; human approval.
8. Blocking and space: SceneSpace coordinates, factions, positions, paths, axes, SVG preview; human lock.
9. Storyboard: depth and view choices, Shot Plan, 1–12-second ShotTimelineSpecs, key moments, optional reference images; human approval.
10. Canonical video prompts: full structured record plus compact per-shot execution prompt.
11. Platform and media: fact-preserving adapter, capability probe, authorized execution or manual recipe.
12. Voice, subtitle, and edit: dialogue, TTS/script, SFX/music, Edit Plan, render or edit package.
13. Final validation and delivery: hashes, media evidence, deterministic ZIP, cover/copy/checklist; no auto-publish.

The numbered folder is the only active business location for each phase. `runtime/` is evidence,
`.history/` is revision/migration history, and Markdown/text/SVG views are deterministic derivatives.
