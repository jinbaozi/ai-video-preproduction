# Phase 10 — Canonical Video Prompts

Compile the approved screenplay, style, asset registry, SceneSpace, and storyboard into a complete
Canonical record plus a compact, independently copyable execution prompt for each shot. Preserve
source paths and hashes; do not load a platform adapter here.

Each named character needs an approved identity image, and every required storyboard moment needs
an approved finished image. Bind actual files/hash/version/slot and inheritance responsibilities.
Reject wrong role versions, stale approvals, whiteboxes, and more than five required video references.
Full execution_controls and field_coverage accompany copyable prompts; hashes alone are not coverage.
