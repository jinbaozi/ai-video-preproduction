# Phase 08 — Blocking and Space

Use approved assets and screenplay to establish scene coordinates, placements, factions, paths,
interaction distances, fixed elements, props, and axis constraints. Deterministic SVG previews are
structured previsualization, never verified 3D. End with explicit human lock approval or revision.

Physical execution uses meters, a right-handed Z-up world. Normalized scenes require explicit
world_bounds; never clamp coordinates into the frame. Include numeric dimensions for fixed geometry,
not only names. Unknown geometry or interactions must be resolved or explicitly limited, not invented.
