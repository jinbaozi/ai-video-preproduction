# Safety and Rights

Record permission for source material and every reference asset before external generation.
Gate real-person likenesses, private people, minors, protected characters/brands, sensitive data,
paid/account actions, uploads, and publication. Unverified rights may continue only as an internal
prompt-only project with external generation disabled.

Do not create deceptive impersonation, non-consensual intimate content, exploitation, or
safeguard-bypass instructions. Preserve attribution and user restrictions in provenance.
